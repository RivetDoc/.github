## Built from experience

The team are former bankers and investors who lived the late-night bakeoff prep and review cycles. We built RivetDoc to remove the friction that slowed us down — instantly search across millions of filings, cite every finding, export any table to Excel, and get real-time alerts the moment new disclosures hit.

## What it does

- **Search** — *Type a phrase. Find every mention. Instantly.* Search every word and number across years of HKEX filings by ticker, phrase, or keyword.
- **Cite** — *Highlight, share, in one click.* Cite the exact passage in the HKEX filing. Audit-ready, traceable, shareable.
- **Extract** — *Pull any table straight into Excel.* No more retyping numbers from PDFs.
- **Track** — *Never miss a disclosure, ever.* Monitor a watchlist; we email the moment a new filing drops.
- **AskRivet AI** — *Research is 80% gathering, 20% analyzing. AskRivet AI flips the ratio.* Every answer cited to the source. No hallucinations.
